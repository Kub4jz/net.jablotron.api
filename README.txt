# Jablotron Cloud

Bring your Jablotron JA-100 alarm system into Homey. Every section of your alarm shows up as its own device, so you can see at a glance whether it is armed, partially armed or disarmed, and switch it with a single tap from anywhere.

Because the alarm becomes part of your smart home, you can put it to work in Flows: arm the house automatically when the last person leaves, disarm it as you pull into the driveway, or get a notification whenever a section changes state.

The app talks to your alarm through the MyJablotron cloud service using your existing MyJablotron account, so Homey needs an internet connection, but there is no extra hardware to install.
